Hello. I'm neutral. This is my corner of the internet, where interesting ideas occasionally collide at unsafe velocities.

I write about power, computers, and the stories we tell to make the machinery seem reasonable. That can mean AI governance, Foucault, Unix, or an unnecessarily elaborate reading of *Zardoz*. Sometimes the digression is the point.

The [archive](/archive/) has all the writing, including pieces from [Neutral Instrumentality](https://neutralzone.substack.com) on Substack. You can also follow along by [RSS](/index.xml).

I build things too. [Labelwatch](https://labelwatch.neutral.zone/) watches ATProto labeling activity over a rolling 30-day window and shows where its coverage runs short. The project and research index lives at [unpingable.com](https://unpingable.com).
