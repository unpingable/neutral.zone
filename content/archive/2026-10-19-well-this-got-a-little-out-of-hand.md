Nothing says *"Sorry I haven't posted in a while"* quite like **"Anyway, I built an operating system."**

About 25 days ago, I was celebrating getting ring-3 userspace execution working in an experimental OS I've been developing called ABSD.

Today, there's a public developer preview with SSH, persistent storage, networking, native applications, and a corresponding source release.

ABSD explores capability-based authority, isolation, explicit failure semantics, and some different ideas about how operating systems should work.

It's also an experiment in AI-assisted systems engineering. I'm directing the architecture, priorities, and acceptance decisions, while AI coding agents have done most of the implementation and testing. The development process has been almost as interesting as the OS itself.

It's *very* much experimental. Not production-ready, not a Linux replacement, and there's plenty left to do.

But it's real, it boots, and you can mess around with it.
